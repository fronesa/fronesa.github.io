Under measurement.

We are re-measuring FD-1 under the Decision Index protocol — the same scope the leading decision models publish against. Results go live when the full two-tier system has been measured, not before.

We publish measured numbers with their protocol and provenance, or we publish nothing. A benchmark page with the wrong scope is worse than no benchmark page.

What we measure

Decisions, not demos.

Yes/no decisions, multiple-choice decisions, and scored decisions — each benchmark run states its dataset, sample size, seed, and exactly which system configuration produced the number. Every figure carries who measured it and under what protocol.

Where a competitor publishes nothing for a benchmark, we say so. Silence is data, and it is never filled in by inference.
